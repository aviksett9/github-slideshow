Method and assumptions

Universe and data

All {n_univ} symbols from NseKit-MCP list_of_fno_stocks. One fno_live_option_chain(symbol, "{expiry}") call per stock every cycle (bid/ask, LTP, OI, volume, NSE IV, underlying value and fetch time in one snapshot). Lot sizes from fno_lot_sizes (DEC-26). Ban list from fno_ban_list. Realised volatility from the last ~60 daily equity_eod_bhavcopy files (close vs NSE previous close). Board meetings and corporate actions to 31-Dec.

Structures

Calls and puts scanned separately. Two-leg 1:2, 1:3, 1:4, 2:3, 2:5. Three-leg 1:2:1 (butterfly / broken wing), 1:3:2, 1:3:1, 2:3:1, 1:4:3, 1:4:2, 2:5:3. Long leg within −10% to +12% of spot, short leg to 30% OTM, wing to 40% OTM, any strike gaps. Whole lots only, at most 5 lots per leg.

Margin

SPAN = worst of NSE's 16 risk-array scenarios for the whole position, less net option value. ELM = NSE's applicable rate (normal 3.5%, deep OTM 5.25%, plus any additional ELM) on short-leg notional. Capital = SPAN + ELM + net debit. Peak funding assumes buy legs first and short premium not yet credited. Delivery margin uses NSE's cash VaR+ELM rate at 10/25/45/70% from E-4 to E-1.

Charges (Dhan tariff as published 23-Nov-2026)

₹20 per executed order (one order per leg; also on expired, exercised or assigned contracts). NSE transaction 0.0355299% of premium. STT 0.15% of sell premium and 0.15% of intrinsic value on exercise. SEBI 0.0001%, stamp 0.003% on buys, IPFT 0.0000001%, GST 18%. Physical delivery: 0.10% brokerage on contract value plus delivery STT 0.1%.

Probabilities and expected value

Forward from put-call parity on near-ATM strikes (NSE futures price from SPAN where quotes were too wide). Black-76 IVs per strike. P(profit) uses the market's own smile. Expected value uses a log-normal around the forward with σ = max(HV20, HV60), so it reflects realised volatility rather than the option prices themselves. Neither is a forecast.

Scoring

Percentile-ranked within each risk group. Defined risk: EV/capital 30%, P(profit) 20%, net max ROI 20%, max loss/capital 15%, liquidity 8%, charges/profit 7%. Unlimited/substantial: EV/capital 25%, breakeven distance in σ 20%, P(profit) 15%, net max ROI 15%, 2.5σ stress loss/capital 15%, liquidity 5%, charges 5%. Debit size never enters a safety score.
