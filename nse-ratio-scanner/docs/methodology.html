NSE Ratio-Spread Scanner

How the Docker service finds call and put ratio spreads, butterflies and broken-wing butterflies on NSE stock options, prices them on live quotes, computes NSE margin and Dhan charges, and decides what to alert. It is an analysis tool. It never places orders.

Realised volatility is the only input built from end-of-day data, because it measures how much the stock actually moved over past days. Every price, breakeven, P&L and margin figure uses the live chain snapshot from the same cycle.

3The scan cycle

  1. Market gate. If NSE's capital market is not open, the cycle is skipped (unless forced with POST /api/scan).
  2. Daily refresh (first cycle of the day): universe, lots, ban list, events, ELM, VaR, missing bhavcopies → volatility.
  3. Expiry. Pick the target expiry (section 4).
  4. SPAN. Probe NSE Clearing for the freshest file (today's end-of-day, then intraday i9…i1, then previous days). Parse only the target expiry's option series when the file is new.
  5. Chains. Fetch every stock's chain for the target expiry (about 1–1.3 s each, within NseKit's built-in 0.35 s rate limit).
  6. Price and score every structure (sections 5–10), rank, deep-dive the top 3, and re-fetch those three chains to confirm the legs still trade near the scanned prices.
  7. Publish the HTML report and JSON, then apply the alert rules (section 11).

Measured on 23-Nov-2026 inside the container: daily refresh about 70 s, SPAN parse 3 s, 218 chains in 3.6 min, pricing, ranking and deep dives about 2 min; 7 minutes in total. If a cycle is still running when the next tick arrives, that tick is skipped.

4Expiry selection

TARGET_EXPIRY=auto (default) takes the first monthly stock-option expiry at least MIN_DTE days away (default 7). Today's expiry is never scanned. On 23-Nov-2026 (November expiry day) it selects 29-Dec-2026 and keeps it until 22-Dec, then rolls to January, so positions are never suggested inside the physical-settlement window. A fixed date such as TARGET_EXPIRY=29-Dec-2026 pins the expiry and falls back to auto once it is within MIN_DTE days.

6Strategy universe

Two legs: buy a, sell b

1:2, 1:3, 1:4, 2:3, 2:5. The long leg is nearer the money; the shorts are further out-of-the-money. Net short options, so risk is uncapped (calls) or large (puts).

Three legs: buy a, sell b, buy c

1:2:1 (butterfly; broken-wing when gaps differ), 1:3:2, 1:3:1, 2:3:1, 1:4:3, 1:4:2, 2:5:3. Defined risk when a + c ≥ b.

Calls and puts are scanned separately. Strike positions are searched independently of the ratio: long leg from −10% to +12% moneyness (in the out-of-the-money direction), shorts up to 30% out, wings up to 40% out, with any strike gaps. Ratios are whole lots, at most 5 lots per leg; one order per leg. A vectorised pre-filter keeps the best few hundred per stock and side (by ROI, expected value, probability, lowest debit and stress loss) before the detailed calculations, which removes duplicates and pointless lot sizes.

7Pricing and payoff

Buys are priced at the ask and sells at the bid. A strike with no two-sided quote is not used, so no figure is an LTP estimate. Net premium is Σ lots × lot size × price (positive = debit).

The expiry payoff is piecewise linear with kinks only at strikes. It is evaluated exactly at every chain strike, at zero and far above the top strike. That gives exact breakevens, maximum profit and the price where it occurs, and maximum loss. A call structure with more shorts than longs is marked unlimited, and no finite maximum loss is ever shown for it. A put structure with net shorts is marked substantial, with its loss at a stock price of zero. The dangerous breakeven is the one beyond the short strikes.

8Forward, implied volatility and the smile

F = median over near-ATM strikes of [ K + (Call_mid − Put_mid) · e^(rT) ] r = 7% (NSE SPAN file rate) Fallback when quotes are too wide: F = SPAN December futures price × (live spot / SPAN spot) IV per strike: Black-76 on F, solved from the mid price; smile = out-of-the-money side at each strike

Using the forward instead of spot matters when a dividend or event is priced in. On 23-Nov-2026, HYUNDAI's forward sat 3.4% below cost of carry. Pricing off spot with one ATM IV would have understated its upside risk. A forward more than 1% away from carry is flagged in every report row.

9Margin and capital

Margin is computed from NSE Clearing's own SPAN file, so hedge benefits come from the official risk scenarios rather than a rule of thumb. For a position with signed quantities qi (shares, + long) and risk arrays RAi,s (loss per share of a long, 16 scenarios s):

Scan risk = max(0, max_s Σ q_i · RA_i,s) Option value NOV = Σ q_i · p_i (SPAN-file option prices) SPAN required = max(0, Scan risk − NOV) ELM = Σ over short legs |q_i| · Spot · ELM rate (3.5% normal, 5.25% deep OTM, + any additional ELM) Total capital = max(SPAN required + ELM + Net premium, ELM + max(Net premium, 0)) Peak funding = Long premium paid + SPAN required + ELM (buy legs first, before short premium is credited)

Short-option minimum charge is zero in the current NSE file, and calendar-spread charges do not apply to single-expiry positions. Physical-settlement margin is estimated as 10%, 25%, 45% and 70% of the NSE cash VaR+ELM rate on the net in-the-money delivery value from E-4 to E-1, with full delivery value due at expiry.

Unverified against your broker. These figures follow NSE's published files, but Dhan's live basket margin was not available to the scanner. A low debit never implies low capital: on 23-Nov-2026 an SBIN 1:2 call ratio cost a ₹1,200 debit but needed about ₹1.29 lakh of capital. Check every position in your broker's basket before trading.

11Probability, expected value and stress

  • P(profit), market-implied. P(ST > X) ≈ N(d2(F, X, σ(X))) using each strike's own smile IV, integrated over the payoff. This uses the market's own risk-neutral pricing, so it is not a forecast.
  • Expected net P&L. Log-normal around the forward with σ = max(HV20, HV60), minus round-trip charges. A positive value means the structure is cheap relative to how much the stock has actually been moving.
  • Stress. P&L at a 2.5σ and 3σ adverse move, with σ = max(ATM IV, realised, smile IV 15% out-of-the-money on that side).
  • Pre-expiry. Mark-to-market at 14 and 7 days before expiry by Black-76 with the smile held fixed by strike, plus a second case with every IV 5 points higher.

14Adjustment engine

For each top setup the scanner reprices four plans with the current smile and SPAN arrays, and recomputes margin, breakevens, payoff and charges after each one. These are indicative plans, not a guaranteed recovery.

  1. Buy back the excess short lots when spot reaches the short strike with 14 days left (leaves a defined-risk vertical).
  2. Add long wings beyond the short strike at that trigger (turns the ratio into a broken-wing butterfly).
  3. Convert to defined risk at entry by adding the wing at today's ask.
  4. Exit everything if spot reaches halfway from the short strike to the dangerous breakeven (14 and 7 days left), or at 50% of maximum loss for capped structures.

15Limitations

  • Probabilities and expected values are model outputs from IV and realised volatility. They are not predictions.
  • Stock options settle physically. Positions should be closed before E-4; holding into expiry needs cash or shares for delivery.
  • Quotes come from one snapshot per stock, taken over about five minutes. Each stock's legs share one timestamp, but different stocks may differ by a few minutes.
  • NseKit-MCP reads NSE's public website. Site changes or IP blocking cause fetch failures, which are reported per stock rather than filled in.
  • Dhan charges are hard-coded and must be updated if the tariff changes. Margin is unverified against the broker.

16Running it on your laptop

Requirements: Docker Desktop (or Docker Engine), about 1 GB RAM and 1 GB disk, and outbound HTTPS to www.nseindia.in and nsearchives.nseindia.com. The laptop must be awake during market hours for scans to run.
